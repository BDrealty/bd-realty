8 Practical Tips for Buying Property in Mexico

Benjamin Brasher
Updated 23 November 2026 · 1 min read

The buyers who fare best in Mexico are the most patient, the most documented and the most properly advised.

  1. Retain your own attorney. Independent from the seller's and the developer's.
  2. Commission a home inspection. Before closing, not after.
  3. Obtain your RFC early. Your Mexican tax ID, needed for ownership, banking and tax filings.
  4. Verify the utilities. Water, electricity and internet should be connected, current and in the seller's name.
  5. Research the developer. For pre-construction, check delivery history. Delays of 12 to 24 months are not unusual.
  6. Understand your visa. Owning property does not grant residency. Explore Residente Temporal or Permanente.
  7. Open a Mexican bank account. It simplifies utilities, taxes, HOA payments and rental income.
  8. Get the timeline in writing. Before you sign any promissory contract.
Speed is rarely the friend of a sound acquisition in Mexico.

Questions

Does buying property in Mexico give me residency?

No. Owning property does not grant residency. Explore Residente Temporal or Permanente.
