About this property

At this price point, with this address, Distrito Xcalacoco may be the single best value opportunity on the Riviera Maya coast right now. Developed by TM Real Estate Group — the same group behind The Fives, one of the world's most acclaimed luxury all-inclusive resorts — the development sits adjacent to that world-renowned property, inheriting its reputation, its infrastructure, and its standard of delivery. Fifty-six years in business. Over 20,000 homes delivered across Mexico. The track record is not in question. Residences are designed by Tellez Tellez Aretollo Arquitectos, with 40+ years of international luxury hotel and residential experience: modern lines, floor-to-ceiling windows, natural materials, and cross-ventilation engineered into every unit. The amenity programme is resort-grade: pool, 25-metre lap pool, outdoor Jacuzzi, water park, wellness centre, gym, co-working, signature bar, and restaurants. Owners reach their private beach club by golf cart — included as part of daily life here. Delivery November 2026. Entry from $243,000. For buyers who understand what surrounds this development, that number does not last.

Why we recommend it

The Fives is world-renowned. Being developed by the same group, on the same corridor, with a private beach club and resort amenities at this entry price — there are very few opportunities on this coast that offer this combination. Buyers who move now will understand why when they see where pricing goes.
