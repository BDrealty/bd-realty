About this property

Puerto Cancún is the most coveted address in Cancún — marina, championship golf, private beach, five-star dining, and a standard of living no other part of the city offers. Aria puts you inside that world at the most compelling entry point in the estate. The tower itself makes a statement: a nautical sail-shaped façade designed by Artigas Arquitectos and Kobi Karp of Miami, 99 residences spanning 190 to 600 m², a private pier with 12 boat slips, residents' cinema, spa, and resort pool. But what sets Aria apart is the arithmetic. SLS and Shark Tower carry the brand premium. Aria carries the same Puerto Cancún address, the same estate infrastructure — golf, beach, marina, dining — at a price that makes the comparison obvious. For the buyer who knows what Puerto Cancún is worth and wants to enter it intelligently, Aria is the answer.
