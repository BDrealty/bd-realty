About this property

Imagine waking up to an unobstructed panorama of the Caribbean Sea, Isla Mujeres, and the Bay of Cancún — from a private terrace so large it becomes a room of its own. The Residences at The St. Regis Costa Mujeres are designed around that view, with residences from 235 m² to 900 m² penthouses, each with expansive balconies that make the horizon the architecture. This is ultra-luxury living at its most elemental: space, light, and one of the most breathtaking vistas in the Mexican Caribbean. Designed by Sordo Madaleno — Mexico City's most celebrated architectural firm — every detail reflects the St. Regis standard: 24/7 Butler Service, dedicated housekeeping, oceanfront infinity pools, private marina, and personalised cabana service on the beachfront. The development also houses the first SHA-designed Spa in the Americas, with residents enrolled in an invitation-only membership at the SHA Wellness Clinic Mexico — the world's leading longevity clinic — just steps away. Costa Mujeres is the fastest-growing ultra-luxury corridor in the region. Delivery 2026. Very few residences remain.
