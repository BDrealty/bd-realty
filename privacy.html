Privacy notice

What we do with the information you share with us, in plain language.

Who we are

B&D Realty is a real estate agency in the Riviera Maya, Mexico, run by Benjamin Brasher and Jennifer Duzant. We are responsible for the personal information you share with us through this website, WhatsApp or email.

Contact for anything about your data: benjamin@bdrealty.mx.

What we collect

Only what you choose to give us: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and whatever you tell us in a form or message, such as your budget, preferred location, timeline or details of a property you want to sell.

We do not ask for bank details, identity documents or other sensitive information through this website.

Why we use it

To reply to you, send the Buyer's Guide you asked for, share properties that match what you are looking for, arrange calls and viewings, and, if you joined the Investors Circle, tell you about new opportunities.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything you would not expect from those purposes.

Who else sees it

Benjamin and Jennifer. The services that run this website and its forms process your information on our behalf: the website host and the service that receives form submissions, and WhatsApp when you choose to message us there. Some of these providers store data outside Mexico, including in the United States.

When you go ahead with a purchase, sale or rental, we share what is needed with the people involved in that transaction (for example the developer, the owner, the notary or a lawyer), and only with your agreement.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies. Your browser stores two small preferences so the site remembers them: your language choice and whether you closed the Buyer's Guide banner. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and maps from Google Maps, which receive your IP address to deliver them.

If we add visitor statistics in the future, we will update this notice first.

How long we keep it

For as long as we are in contact or working together, and afterwards only as long as we need it for our records. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop us using it (your ARCO rights under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties). Visitors from the European Union and the United Kingdom have equivalent rights.

Email benjamin@bdrealty.mx with your request. We will reply within 20 business days.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date below.
